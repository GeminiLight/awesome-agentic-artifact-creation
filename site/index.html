    Audit and inclusion.

    This catalog tracks systems that plan, generate, inspect, revise, or maintain an artifact through agentic interaction.

    Every public entry is derived from an auditable CSV ledger and a controlled taxonomy. Published venue links are preferred when a formal version exists; uncertain or out-of-scope candidates stay outside the public paper list.
