Interactive framework

A live construction process.

Follow the ordered path from task and policy through representation and verification to the artifact, then back to the next task.

Artifact becomes the next task

An interactive three-dimensional construction process. A task specification enters the construction policy, which acts on an operational representation. Runtime verification observes the result, accepts a delivered artifact, and the artifact can begin the next task cycle.

Survey synthesis

The catalog maps the field. The survey explains what matters.

Reliable creation depends less on workflow size than on keeping commitments, state, evidence, and repair inspectably connected.

Decision Interdependence Failure Observability Diagnosis Repairability

Agency

More agents do not create more agency.

Agency appears when intermediate evidence can redirect later construction decisions, not when a workflow simply adds roles or stages.

Observation Decision

Feedback

A score is not yet actionable feedback.

Feedback becomes operational when evidence names a criterion, localizes affected state, and reaches a feasible repair or escalation.

Evidence Repair

Validity

Accepted evidence has a version.

When requirements, sources, tools, or artifact state change, dependent approvals should expire before selective revalidation.

Change Revalidation

Proportionality

Control should match construction difficulty.

Stronger explicit control helps when decisions are interdependent, failures are hard to observe, or diagnosis does not map cleanly to repair.
